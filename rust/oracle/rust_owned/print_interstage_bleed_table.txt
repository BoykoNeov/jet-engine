
Interstage bleed (rung 42): the device rungs 36 and 41 both deferred, fitted to the
spool rung 41 showed is exposed. A fraction b is extracted at station 25 and dumped --
the project's FIRST steady mass EXTRACTION -- the first time mass LEAVES the flowpath,
so the two COMPRESSORS pass different air. (NOT 'compressor and turbine differ': (1+f)
has done that since rung 2. What is new is mass leaving, not mass changing.)
Comparison held at FIXED Tt4: the valve sets b, not the throttle.

  WHERE b ENTERS -- exactly three places:
    (1) the LP shaft balance   h_c(Tt25)-h_c(Tt2) = eta_m(1-b)(1+f)dh_LPT   => Tt25 FALLS
    (2) the LP face referral   (ddagger-b): mdot_corr,2 picks up an explicit 1/(1-b)
    (3) the thrust books       the dumped air keeps full ram drag, returns no momentum
  and NOT the HP face: rung 39's (dagger) mdot_corr,25 = A4 pi_b pi_HPC MFP* sqrt(Tt25/Tt4)/(1+f)
  is core flow on BOTH sides, so it carries NO b -- which is why _hp_eta_loop is reused
  VERBATIM. Its BODY is b-free; its ARGUMENTS are not (rung 39's leaf, one rung on).

  THE TRADE. Thrust falls 10.0% -> 14.7% and TSFC rises 6.3% -> 14.6% (b = 0.10) as
  the throttle comes back: the valve gets MORE SELECTIVE and MORE EXPENSIVE together --
  which is why real bleed is SCHEDULED, not simply left open. And bleed lowers pi_LPC
  hence pt4, so it SHRINKS the choked envelope (lowest runnable Tt4 605 -> 630 K over
  b = 0 -> 0.15): the inherited nozzle-choke guard bites sooner. It flags, it never lies.

  A HYPOTHESIS, REFUTED and kept visible (rung 40's convention): this rung was
  proposed as 'bleed protects the LP AT THE HP SPOOL'S EXPENSE'. FALSE -- above pi* the
  HP flow coefficient RISES too, just 10-100x less; below pi* it falls, by ~1e-4. The
  textbook trade is not what the choked two-spool hardware does.
