  A different arrow from rung 42's. The HP turbine now drives its compressor with less gas than
  the compressor pumps, so the HP spool gets a new freedom - pushed AWAY from surge at every power,
  with no sign change. The LP spool is pushed TOWARD surge at high power (the HP's pressure-ratio
  drop starves the LP face more than the missing air feeds it) and helped below ~910 K. The slam's
  closest approach sits on the high-power side, so the LP protection valve must bleed MORE to hold
  the same margin, its ceiling falls, and a fixed AMOUNT of air - a larger share at the slam's
  low-power start - costs more than the same share. Customer air also costs more thrust and fuel
  than interstage air: it carries the HP compressor's work as well.
