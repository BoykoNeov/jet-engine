  At EQUAL protection the phi valve bleeds 1.3-3.5x more and costs more thrust: it holds a
  CONSTANT flow coefficient, so it over-protects early in the ramp where the stators are most
  closed, to be safe late where they have reopened. The incidence valve opens only as far as
  each moment needs -- and the stators pay part of its bill (it bleeds less with them than
  without), where the phi valve fights them (more). Beside a phi FUEL floor the incidence
  valve lets phi fall and the floor starves the run: watch the same wall with both.
