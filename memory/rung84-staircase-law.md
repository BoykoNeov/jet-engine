Rung 84 (shipped 2026-08-12) answered rung 83's `(r, ds)` map seam. The physical verdict is in
CLAUDE.md's table; these are the **process** lessons.

**A SMALL INTEGER COUNT CANNOT CARRY A RATE — and it killed TWO of seven predictions.** P3 was a
ratio of jump counts ("doubles ±25% per halving"); P5's `Λ` divided by a tread estimated as
*window / count*. The counts were **1, 3, 5**, where the ±1 quantization inherent to counting grid
crossings is ±100% and ±33% — so neither bar could have passed on a correct plant. Measured 3.00 /
1.67 / **2.00 / 1.90**: the doubling appears only once the count reaches 5. The repair was to test
the **invariant** instead (`n·ds` = one plant number, five levels over 16×, consistency bands
intersecting) — which confirmed the content the ratio bar could not reach. This is the **third**
bar in this lineage to die of its own wording, after rung 82's P1 and rung 83's P5; the earlier two
named a *direction* with no point, this pair named a *quantity coarser than the effect*. Check a
bar's resolution against the effect before registering it. See [[rung83-corrector-law]],
[[rung82-threshold-law]].

**THE HEADLINE WAS WRITTEN BEFORE THE MEASUREMENT THAT REFUTED IT.** "Refining the march RELOCATES
a root rather than creating one" went into the anchor's § 1.3, the class docstring and the planned
spec — then P6 measured **absent / present / present / present** over four steps and refuted it.
The advisor blocked the spec on exactly this ("do not write until P6 lands") and was right. The
derivation (shadow *fraction* is `ds`-free) is sound; the *consequence* it suggested is a claim
about a **rate**, and one ramp over four steps cannot test one. **A derivation licenses a
mechanism, not a trend.**

**A DEFECTIVE ESTIMATOR WAS IN THE SHIPPED READER, not just the probe.** `staircase_number`
derived its own tread from a quantized count, so the spec's headline number would have silently
corrected the shipped method's output — slice 4's "characterises an ADJACENT computation" trap.
Fixed by making `spacing` an **argument** that returns no `lam` when absent, so the choice sits in
the signature. See [[golden-gate-slice4]].

**THE PRE-CHECK PAID FOR ITSELF IN FOUR MARCHES.** One line of algebra — *a minimum over a FIXED
finite set of continuous functions is continuous, so it kinks and cannot jump* — said rung 83's
stated mechanism named the wrong object before any sweep ran. Four marches confirmed it (99.4%
membership term vs exactly 0.0 at the control), which replaced a planned ~56-minute two-parameter
sweep with targeted probes and turned the rung's classifier from a **ratio judgement** into an
**identity**. Fifth rung running where a pre-check settled the foundation before the anchor existed
([[rung81-authority-clock]], [[rung82-threshold-law]]).

**AND THE CROSS-RUNG CLAIM WAS BOUNDED BEFORE IT WAS MEASURED.** The anchor wrote down, in advance,
that rung 83's `argmin_moved` flag fired *correctly* and reported a *consequence* — verdict
CONFIRMED, reason CORRECTED — explicitly to prevent the "rung 83 was wrong" over-claim that rungs
63 and 71 are on record for. P4 then refuted its own prediction into the stronger reading (0
counter-examples ⇒ the flags are coextensive **by construction**), which the anchor had also
pre-registered as an outcome. Registering both outcomes is what let the refutation be content.
