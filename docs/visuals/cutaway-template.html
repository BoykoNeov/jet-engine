Reading the picture

The geometry is schematic. Everything the colors and readouts say is computed by the model.

    Stations follow the usual numbering: 0 free stream, 2 compressor face, 3 compressor exit, 4 turbine inlet, 5 turbine exit, 9 nozzle exit. Numbers come from the turbojet package run at the design point by docs/visuals/extract_data.py.

    Real-components losses: π_d 0.97 · η_c 0.88 · η_b 0.99 · π_b 0.96 · η_t 0.90 · η_m 0.99 · π_n 0.98. Parcel speeds, blade counts and the primary-zone temperature are illustrative; the primary zone is drawn hotter than the 1500 K turbine inlet because the combustor burns near stoichiometric before dilution, which is what the zoned-combustor rungs model.
