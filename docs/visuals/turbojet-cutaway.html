Reading the picture

The geometry is schematic. The station readouts, the performance table and the T–s diagram are the model’s own numbers; the colors interpolate between those station values. The footer says which is which.

    Stations follow the usual numbering: 0 free stream, 2 compressor face, 3 compressor exit, 4 turbine inlet, 5 turbine exit, 9 nozzle exit. Numbers come from the turbojet package run at the design point by docs/visuals/extract_data.py; the flight and design condition above, the losses below and every station and performance figure on this page are read from that run, not typed in.

    What is the model's, and what is drawn. The model's: the six station readouts (Tt, pt, f), the performance table, the T–s diagram, the exhaust velocity and exit Mach, and the flight and design condition. Drawn: all geometry and proportions, the eight compressor and two turbine stages, the shaft speed, the parcel speeds and streak lengths, and the primary-zone flame temperature — the cycle carries one mixed-out Tt4, so a hotter near-stoichiometric primary zone is the zoned-combustor rungs' picture, illustrated here rather than solved. The hover probe reports the model's value at a station and a smooth interpolation between stations, so read it as a profile, not as a solved field. Parcel color is a fixed 250–2200 K ramp.
