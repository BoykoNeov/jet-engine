## 4. The injection table — what the ORACLE catches that the 59 gates do not

Step 3 measured the six injections against the four ported suites. Two of them —
**I1** (the locally-armed-core carrier) and **I2** (`arm`'s HP branch deleted) — moved
**0 of 302** gate-visible probe readings and were caught by **0 of 59** gates. Re-run here
against `slice_v_oracle.rs`:

| injection | caught by the 59 gates (step 3) | oracle status | oracle keys differing |
|---|---|---|---|
| `I1_local_armed_core` | **0 / 59** | CAUGHT | **87** / 6819 |
| `I2_hp_arm_dropped` | **0 / 59** | CAUGHT | **709** / 6819 |
| `I3_smooth_shape_cubed` | 4 / 59 | CAUGHT | **1945** / 6819 |
| `I4_erosion_inverted` | 5 / 59 | CAUGHT | **14** / 6819 |
| `I5_incidence_lever_sign` | 5 / 59 | PANIC-BEFORE-COMPARE | n/a |
| `I6_arm_reads_the_wrong_shaft` | 4 / 59 | CAUGHT | **1440** / 6819 |

**`I5` is caught by a PANIC, not by a key.** Inverting the incidence lever's sign empties
a trajectory and `refine_min` indexes `traj[0]`, so the run dies before the comparator
sees anything — which is why its row reads `0 / 0`. The harness gives that its OWN status
rather than letting it read as *nothing moved*: slice S step 3's lesson (*an injection
reporting "nothing moved" could not have moved anything*) applied to the instrument.

**THIS DOES NOT DISCHARGE P5.** The oracle detects I1 at 87 keys, and P5 still stands,
because the two gates fail differently: a golden-comparison gate is defeated by
regenerating the golden against buggy code, and a gate that MANUFACTURES the scoped-arm
behaviour inline is not. Step 5 still owes the manufactured-carrier gate; what step 4
removes from its list is only the *call-order* problem — see § 5.
